# Risk and data quality

- Keep source, retrieval time, version, method, confidence, fallback status,
  and limitations attached to derived risk data.
- Use deterministic fixtures for hazard, exposure, loss, cluster, and model
  benchmark changes.
- Distinguish screening proxies from catastrophe models, engineering reports,
  or underwriting decisions.
- Validate coordinates, dates, units, caps, and missing-data behavior at the
  boundary.
- Update `docs/risk-model.md` and the changelog when methodology changes.
